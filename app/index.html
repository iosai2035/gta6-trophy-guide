The road to Vice Got a Price!

One playthrough is enough — there is no mission replay and no New Game+, so play the story once and clean up in free-roam after the credits. Expect about 200–250 hours in all (PowerPyx says ~200, Push Square 250+). No difficulty settings exist, there are no online trophies, and the 61 cheats disable trophies and saving — keep trophy runs cheat-free.

  1. Step 1 — Play the 5 chapters 60–80 hours

    Do the optional missions as they appear, scan vehicles and study animals on the way, and keep a manual save at the start of each chapter (15 manual saves plus one autosave). The only thing to watch for: the Chapter 1 meditation for Positive Mental Attitude — see the warning above.

    Earns: the 12 story trophies (4 chapter completions, 7 mission trophies, the final-mission Gold) as you go.

  2. Step 2 — After the credits, work the 100% checklist ~100 hours

    The checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Most of its 35 requirements ask for only part of a total, so it is closer to 60% true completion — but the no-slack items must be done in full: all robberies, 33 underwater discoveries, 42 shooting-range golds, 42 drone time trials and Bret's 15 Target Decks.

    Earns: A Long Road (Gold) and most of the activity, crime and collectible trophies.

  3. Step 3 — Strangers, side quests and leftovers ~10 hours

    Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands and the 6 side-character finales, then clean up whatever is left — the one-off trophies and any activity counts still short.

    Earns: Strangers & Freaks, the 6 side finales, the hidden one-offs — and then the Platinum.

Missable trophies

Only one trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude (Chapter 1 meditation at Jason's house, after “Down to Earth”). Everything else — story included — can still be earned in free-roam after the credits. For the 100% checklist only 15 of the 18 meditations are needed, so the missable one cannot block A Long Road.

Chapter order

  • Chapter 1 — Just Like Paradise, Paradise Lost (plus the Chapter 1 meditation — do it now)
  • Chapter 2 — T**s Ass Drug Money, Trust (Breakfast Run) (partner play unlocks at “Coffee Break”)
  • Chapter 3 — Vice City, Baby!, Break the Bank (The Cartel Heist)
  • Chapter 4 — Top Of The World, Ma; Family Matters, DWNFALL, Hi-Jacked (bank vaults open here)
  • Chapter 5 — Snow Storm (Operation Savage Glory), then the final mission (Gold)
